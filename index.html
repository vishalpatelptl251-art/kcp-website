Questions buyers ask

What do I need to give you for a quote?

Your site location, area or quantity, the application and the expected load. Photos or drawings help.

Do you supply kerb stones with pavers?

Yes. Most road, driveway and parking jobs need both, and we can quote them together.

Can I get test reports?

Ask for the strength test report for the batch you’re buying, and for the standards it is made to.

Which colours and grades do you make?

Pavers come in grey, red, black, yellow and green, except grass pavers. Grades are M30, M40 and M50, made to your project requirement.

What is the minimum order?

It depends on the product. Message us your quantity.

Do you deliver?

Yes, around Bengaluru. Share your site location for delivery planning and charges.

Can I see the blocks first?

You’re welcome at our Dasanapura factory on Tumkur Road. Call ahead so we’re ready for you.

Are the finishes and patterns made using PVC moulds?

We use PVC moulds for concrete products and offer the catalogue patterns shown. Ask us about the available face finish for your selected product. Actual colour and finish can vary between production batches.
